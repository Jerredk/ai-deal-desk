1. Solution Overview

What problem does this solve?

Enterprise deals often stall in commercial review: discount authority, payment terms, margin floors, and non-standard legal clauses must be checked before a quote can move. Manual deal-desk review is slow, inconsistent and hard to scale. This application automates a first-pass review so RevOps and deal desks can focus on exceptions.

Note: Approval authority stays with leadership. Rather than automating sign-off, the agent delivers a decision-ready brief covering verified margins, required approval lanes, contract risk flags, and an executive summary.

What was built

One deal-desk agent pipeline, three configurations: version 1 baseline, version 2 guardrailed, and version 3 with a critic.

  • Code owns exact calculations: margin, penalties and approval lanes.
  • The AI Agent handles unstructured judgment: reading redlines and non-standard clauses, choosing risk flags, and drafting the executive memo.
