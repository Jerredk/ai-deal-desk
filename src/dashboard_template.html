1. Solution Overview

A deal-desk agent that reviews enterprise SaaS commercial terms, calculates margin and approval requirements, grounds risk findings in policy, and produces a structured executive memo for sales leadership.

What problem does this solve?

Enterprise deals often stall in commercial review: discount authority, payment terms, margin floors, and non-standard legal clauses must be checked before a quote can move. Manual deal-desk review is slow and inconsistent. This application automates a first-pass review so RevOps and deal desks can focus on exceptions.

The agent does not replace human approval. It prepares a structured recommendation — calculated margin, required approval tier, detected risk flags, and an executive memo — for a human decision-maker.
